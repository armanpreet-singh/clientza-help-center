---
title: "How to Create an Appointment"
description: "Schedule a new appointment with the right service, provider, and time."
---

Create appointments from the Appointments or Calendar workflows in the Owner app.

## Before you begin

- Confirm the customer record exists.
- Confirm at least one service and provider are set up.

## Steps

1. Open Appointments or Calendar.
2. Select the option to create a new appointment.
3. Choose the customer.
4. Choose the service.
5. Choose the provider.
6. Select an available date and time.
7. Review details and save.

## Expected outcome

The appointment appears in your appointment list or calendar with its current status.

## Troubleshooting

### No times are available

Review business hours, date overrides, provider assignment, and scheduling conflicts.

See [Troubleshoot Unavailable Appointment Times](/appointments/troubleshoot-unavailable-times).

### Appointment stays pending

Review [Understand Appointment Statuses](/appointments/understand-appointment-statuses).
