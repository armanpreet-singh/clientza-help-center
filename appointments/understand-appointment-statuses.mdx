Appointment statuses help you track where each booking is in its lifecycle.

## Common statuses

Statuses can vary by your current product version and workflow, but owners commonly see statuses such as:

- Pending
- Confirmed
- Cancelled
- Completed

Always rely on the labels shown directly in your Owner app.

## How to use statuses

1. Open Appointments or Calendar.
2. Check each appointment status label.
3. Prioritize actions for pending or changed bookings.
4. Use available actions to confirm, reschedule, or cancel.

## Expected outcome

You can understand booking state quickly and take the right next action.

## Troubleshooting

### I am not sure why an appointment is pending

Check whether all required booking details were finalized and whether confirmation actions are still needed.

### A status changed unexpectedly

Refresh the page and review recent actions by your team.
