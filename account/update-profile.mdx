---
title: "How to Update Your Profile"
description: "Update your owner account profile information in Clientza."
---

Keep your account profile current so your business records stay accurate.

## Steps

1. Open your account or profile page.
2. Update available profile fields.
3. Save your changes.
4. Reopen the page to confirm values persisted.

## Expected outcome

Your profile fields update successfully in your account view.

## Important note

Some profile preferences may save without changing behavior across the entire app immediately.

Treat preferences as saved account settings unless your current interface explicitly confirms wider app impact.
