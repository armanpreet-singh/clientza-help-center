---
title: "How to Add a Customer"
description: "Create customer records so you can schedule and manage appointments."
---

Add customer details before creating appointments for them.

## Steps

1. Open the Customers page.
2. Select the option to add a customer.
3. Enter required customer details.
4. Save the record.

## Expected outcome

The customer appears in your customer list and can be selected in appointment flows.

## Troubleshooting

### Customer record does not save

Check required fields and correct validation errors.

### I entered the wrong details

Use the available edit controls if shown in your current app.

If edit controls are not available in your interface, keep a note of the correction needed and use the supported workflow currently available to your team.
