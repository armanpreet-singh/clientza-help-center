---
title: "How to Review Customer-Reported Payments"
description: "Review payment records, status, and details before marking payments as settled."
---

Use the Payments page to review payment entries and confirm their current status.

## Steps

1. Open Payments.
2. Find the payment you want to review.
3. Open its details.
4. Check status and payment method.
5. Confirm details before taking action.

## Expected outcome

You can identify which payments are pending and which have already been marked paid.

## Troubleshooting

### Payment remains pending

Confirm whether the customer-reported payment has been verified in your process, then update status if appropriate.

### I cannot find a payment

Review filters and date ranges in the Payments page.
