---
title: "How to Create a Communication Draft"
description: "Prepare communication drafts in the Owner app for later review."
---

Use drafts to prepare outbound communication content before final action.

## Steps

1. Open Communications.
2. Select create draft.
3. Enter the message details requested by the form.
4. Save the draft.

## Expected outcome

Your draft appears in the communications list with its current status.

## Important note

This guide covers draft-management workflows.

Do not assume send and delivery workflows are available unless your current owner interface provides and confirms them.
