---
title: "How to Assign Providers to Services"
description: "Link providers to services so appointments can be scheduled with the right team members."
---

Provider assignment connects each service to the people who can deliver it.

## Before you begin

- Confirm the service exists.
- Confirm at least one provider has been added.

## Steps

1. Open the service you want to update.
2. Find the provider assignment section.
3. Select one or more providers.
4. Save the service.

## Expected outcome

The service is linked to selected providers and can be scheduled accordingly.

## Troubleshooting

### No providers are available to assign

Add providers first in the Providers section.

### Assigned providers are not retained

Save again and check for validation errors or missing required fields.
