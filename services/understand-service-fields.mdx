title: "Understand Service Fields"
description: "Learn what each common service field means so you can configure services correctly."
---

When creating or editing services, you may see several fields that affect scheduling and display.

## Common fields

- Service name: What owners and staff see in service lists.
- Category: Grouping used to organize services.
- Duration: Time block used for appointment scheduling.
- Price: Amount shown for service cost where pricing is used.
- Provider assignment: Who can perform the service.
- Status: Whether the service is active and available for use.

## How to use fields well

1. Use clear service names.
2. Keep durations realistic to avoid scheduling conflicts.
3. Review pricing for consistency before saving.
4. Confirm provider assignment after updates.

## Expected outcome

Service records are easier to maintain, and appointment setup is clearer.

## Troubleshooting

### I am not sure which fields are required

Follow the validation messages in the form. Required fields usually show inline errors when missing.

### A field is not visible on my screen

Some fields can vary by account configuration. Use only fields that are shown and editable in your Owner app.
