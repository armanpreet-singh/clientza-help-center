---
title: "How to Find a Customer"
description: "Search and locate customer records quickly in the Owner app."
---

Use the Customers page to locate records before booking or reviewing history.

## Steps

1. Open the Customers page.
2. Use search or filters if they are available.
3. Enter customer details such as name or contact info.
4. Open the matching record.

## Expected outcome

You can quickly find the right customer profile.

## Troubleshooting

### Search returns no results

Check spelling, remove extra spaces, and try broader search terms.

### I still cannot find the customer

Confirm the customer was saved correctly. If needed, add a new record using [How to Add a Customer](/customers/add-customer).
