---
title: "How to Create and Edit a Service"
description: "Add new services, update service details, and keep your service list accurate."
---

Create services to define what customers can book.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to the Owner app.
- Open the Services page.

## Steps to create a service

1. Select the option to create a new service.
2. Enter the required fields shown on screen.
3. Choose a category if categories are enabled.
4. Assign providers if the form includes provider assignment.
5. Save the service.

## Steps to edit a service

1. Find the service in your list.
2. Open the service details.
3. Update the fields you need.
4. Save your changes.

## Expected outcome

Your service appears in the list with updated details and can be used in appointment workflows where applicable.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot save the service

Review required fields and any validation messages.

### I do not see my service in booking flows

Check whether the service is active and whether at least one provider is assigned.
