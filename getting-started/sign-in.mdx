---
title: "How to Sign In to Clientza"
description: "Learn how to access your Clientza Owner account and what to check if you cannot sign in."
---

Sign in to Clientza to access your Owner dashboard and manage your business.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- A Clientza owner account.
- Access to the email address associated with your account.
- Your account password, unless your sign-in flow offers another supported authentication method.

## Step 1: Open the sign-in page

Open the Clientza Owner application and navigate to `/login`.

## Step 2: Enter your account details

Enter the email address and password associated with your Clientza account.

Check that the email address is correct and that you have not accidentally included spaces.

## Step 3: Submit the sign-in form

Click the sign-in button displayed on the page.

If your credentials are accepted, Clientza continues the authentication flow and directs you to the appropriate page for your account.

Depending on your account setup, you may be directed to complete your initial business setup before accessing the Owner dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot sign in

Check that you entered the correct email address and password. Correct any typing mistakes and try again.

### I forgot my password

Open `/forgot-password` in the Clientza Owner application and follow the password-recovery instructions.

For more details, see [How to Reset Your Password](/getting-started/reset-password).

### I have not verified my email

If you have not completed email verification during registration, return to the registration flow and follow the verification instructions provided by Clientza.

Read [How to Verify Your Email Address](/getting-started/verify-email).

### I can sign in, but I cannot access my business dashboard

You may need to complete your initial business setup if you have not created a business workspace yet.

See [How to Complete Your Initial Business Setup](/getting-started/initial-business-setup).

### The problem continues

If your credentials are correct but sign-in still fails, note the message displayed by Clientza. Avoid repeatedly submitting credentials if the application indicates that your account requires additional action.
