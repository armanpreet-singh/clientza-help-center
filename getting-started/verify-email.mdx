# How to Verify Your Email Address

Email verification is part of the Clientza owner registration process. After submitting the registration form, check the email address you entered and complete the verification form.

## When do I need to verify my email?

Verify your email address when you create a new Clientza owner account and the registration process asks you to enter a verification code.

## Before you begin

Make sure you can access the email inbox you used during registration.

## Step 1: Open your email inbox

After submitting the owner registration form, open the inbox for the email address you entered.

Look for the verification email from Clientza.

## Step 2: Find your verification code

Open the verification email and locate the code.

If you cannot find the email, check your Spam or Junk folder. Also, confirm that you entered the correct email address during registration.

## Step 3: Enter the code in Clientza

Return to the Clientza registration flow where the email verification form is displayed.

1. Find the field labeled **Enter verification code**.
2. Enter the code from your email.
3. Click **Verify email**.

Follow any message displayed by Clientza after submitting the code.

## Step 4: Continue registration

After successful verification, Clientza continues the registration and authentication flow.

If you have not created a business workspace, you may be directed to the initial business setup process.

Continue with [How to Complete Your Initial Business Setup](/getting-started/initial-business-setup).

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot find the verification email

Check your Spam or Junk folder and confirm that the email address you entered during registration is correct.

### I entered the code, but verification failed

Check the code against the email you received and correct any typing mistakes. If Clientza continues to reject it, the issue may require further investigation.

### I cannot find an option to resend the code

The inspected registration interface does not display a resend-code control. Do not assume that a resend option is available on another screen unless it is verified in the application.

### I closed the verification screen

The supplied interface code does not establish whether an incomplete verification session can be resumed. Follow the current prompts in Clientza rather than assuming that registration has finished.
