---
title: "How to Mark a Payment as Paid"
description: "Update a payment record from pending to paid after you confirm payment receipt."
---

Mark payments as paid after your business confirms receipt.

## Steps

1. Open Payments.
2. Select the payment record.
3. Choose the action to mark as paid.
4. Confirm the update.

## Expected outcome

The payment status changes to paid in your payment records.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot change the status

Check whether the payment is already in a final status or restricted by the current workflow.

### Status changed back

Refresh and verify the record. Reapply the action if the app still shows it as pending.
