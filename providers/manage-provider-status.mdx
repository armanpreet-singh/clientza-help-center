---
title: "How to Activate or Deactivate a Provider"
description: "Control whether a provider is available for assignment and booking workflows."
---

Use provider status controls to keep your active team list accurate.

## Steps

1. Open the Providers page.
2. Select the provider you want to update.
3. Locate the status control (active or inactive).
4. Apply the change and save.

## Expected outcome

The provider status updates and affects where that provider can be used.

## Troubleshooting

### Status change does not persist

Save again and check for validation errors in other required fields.

### Inactive provider still appears in a workflow

Refresh the page and review service assignments. You may need to remove the provider from specific services.
