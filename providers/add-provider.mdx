---
title: "How to Add and Update Providers"
description: "Create provider profiles and keep provider details up to date."
---

Providers are the people assigned to perform services.

## Steps to add a provider

1. Open the Providers page.
2. Select the option to add a provider.
3. Enter required provider details.
4. Save.

## Steps to update a provider

1. Find the provider in the list.
2. Open the profile.
3. Edit the needed fields.
4. Save changes.

## Expected outcome

Provider details are available for service assignment and appointment scheduling.

## Troubleshooting

### Provider cannot be saved

Check required fields and correct any validation errors.

### Provider does not appear in service assignment

Refresh the service form and confirm the provider record is active and saved.
