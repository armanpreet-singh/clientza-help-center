---
title: "How to Cancel a Communication Draft"
description: "Cancel draft communications that should not be sent or used."
---

Cancel drafts that are no longer needed.

## Steps

1. Open Communications.
2. Find the draft entry.
3. Open available actions.
4. Choose cancel draft.
5. Confirm the action.

## Expected outcome

The draft updates to a cancelled state or is removed from active draft work, based on your app behavior.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot cancel the draft

Some statuses may not allow cancellation. Review [Understand Communication Statuses](/communications/understand-communication-statuses).
