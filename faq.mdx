## How do I get started with Clientza?

Start with [How Clientza Owner Onboarding Works](/getting-started/create-owner-account). Owner onboarding is invitation-based.

## How do I accept my invitation?

Use [How to Accept a Clientza Owner Invitation](/getting-started/accept-owner-invitation).

## What should I do if an invitation link does not work?

Use [Invitation Link Problems](/troubleshooting/invitation-link-problems).

## What if I already have an account?

Use the existing-account path during invitation acceptance when offered, then sign in.

## Why can I not choose an appointment time?

Check business hours, date overrides, provider assignment, and conflicts.

Use [Troubleshoot Unavailable Appointment Times](/appointments/troubleshoot-unavailable-times).

## Why is an appointment pending?

Review [Understand Appointment Statuses](/appointments/understand-appointment-statuses).

## Why is a payment pending?

Payments can remain pending until you confirm and mark them as paid.

Use [Review Customer-Reported Payments](/payments/review-payment).

## What should I do if a settings change does not save?

Use [Settings Not Saving](/troubleshooting/settings-not-saving).

## How do I recover access to my account?

Use [Account Access and Recovery](/account/account-access-and-recovery).
