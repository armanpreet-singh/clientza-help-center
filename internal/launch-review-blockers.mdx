## High-priority blockers

1. Consultation form submission is not connected
   - Impact: Prospective-owner onboarding cannot be documented as complete self-service.
   - Required action: Implement and verify end-to-end consultation submission, acknowledgement, and review workflow.
   - Public docs status: Keep consultation request page out of public navigation.

2. Source-of-truth repositories inaccessible from this docs workspace
   - Impact: Feature verification against current frontend/backend/admin code is incomplete.
   - Required action: Provide read-only access or validated snapshots before final publication approval.

## Potentially incomplete or preview-sensitive workflows

- Availability page behavior versus settings-based persisted schedules must be re-verified.
- Insights and dashboard metrics must be confirmed as live versus preview content.
- Communications send/delivery workflow must be confirmed before documenting sending.
- Security/session/device-management actions must be confirmed before actionable docs.
- Profile preference application scope must be confirmed.
- Customer edit/delete availability must be confirmed before explicit how-to guidance.

## Business-rule confirmations needed

- Invitation expiry and re-invite policy wording.
- Supported account-recovery support channel and escalation language.
- Payment policy language boundaries for pending versus paid records.
- Any customer-facing legal or compliance notices required in help content.

## Publication gate

Do not approve final public launch until all blocker items are resolved or accepted by product/documentation owner with explicit risk sign-off.
