Use this guide when you cannot access your owner account.

## Common access actions

- Sign in with your existing credentials.
- Reset your password when forgotten.
- Complete invitation acceptance when onboarding.

## What to check first

1. Confirm the email address is correct.
2. Re-enter the password carefully.
3. Use reset password if needed.
4. If invited, open the latest invitation email and continue from that link.

## Expected outcome

You recover access to your owner account and can continue your workflow.
