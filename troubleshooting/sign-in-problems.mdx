## Quick checks

1. Confirm email and password spelling.
2. Remove accidental spaces.
3. Retry once with careful re-entry.

## If you forgot your password

Use [How to Reset Your Password](/getting-started/reset-password).

## If you are still onboarding

Complete invitation acceptance first: [How to Accept a Clientza Owner Invitation](/getting-started/accept-owner-invitation).

## If access still fails

Capture the exact error message and timestamp, then share with your support contact.
