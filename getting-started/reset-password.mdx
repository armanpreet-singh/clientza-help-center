---
title: "How to Reset Your Password"
description: "Recover access to your Clientza owner account when you forget your password."
---

Reset your password if you cannot sign in with your current one.

## Before you begin

Make sure you can access the email address linked to your owner account.

## Step 1: Open password recovery

Go to the password recovery screen from sign-in, or open the recovery route shown in the Owner app.

## Step 2: Request a reset

Enter your account email address and submit the form.

Follow the on-screen confirmation.

## Step 3: Open the reset email

Check your inbox for a password reset email from Clientza.

If you do not see it, check Spam or Junk.

## Step 4: Set a new password

Open the reset link and create a new password that satisfies the rules shown on screen.

Confirm the password and submit.

## Step 5: Sign in again

Return to sign-in and use your new password.

See [How to Sign In to Clientza](/getting-started/sign-in).

## Expected outcome

Your password is updated and you can access your account again.

## Troubleshooting

### I did not receive the reset email

Check Spam or Junk, then verify you entered the correct account email.

### The reset link expired

Start the reset flow again and use the newest email link.

### I still cannot sign in

Use [Sign-in Problems](/troubleshooting/sign-in-problems).
