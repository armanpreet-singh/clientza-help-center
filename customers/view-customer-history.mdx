---
title: "View Customer Details and Appointment History"
description: "Open a customer profile to review details and appointment history."
---

Customer profiles help you understand past visits and upcoming bookings.

## Steps

1. Open the Customers page.
2. Find and open the customer record.
3. Review available profile details.
4. Review appointment history shown in the customer view.

## Expected outcome

You can view customer information and history to support scheduling decisions.

## Important note

This guide only covers view and lookup workflows.

Do not assume delete workflows are available unless your interface clearly provides and supports them.

## Troubleshooting

### No appointment history is visible

The customer may not have completed appointments yet, or the appointment may still be in a pending or upcoming state.

### Details appear outdated

Use available update controls if your screen provides them. If not, follow your current team process for corrections.
