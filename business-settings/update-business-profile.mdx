---
title: "How to Update Your Business Profile"
description: "Update your business name, contact information, address, logo, and brand colors in the Clientza Owner dashboard."
---

Keep your business information up to date so you can manage your workspace details from the Clientza Owner dashboard.

## Before you begin

Sign in to your Clientza Owner account and open the business settings page.

## Step 1: Open business settings

In the Owner dashboard, navigate to `/settings`.

The settings page contains your business profile and other workspace configuration options.

## Step 2: Review your business information

Find the business profile fields available on the settings page.

Depending on the fields displayed for your workspace, you can update:

- **Business name:** The name associated with your business workspace.
- **Contact information:** The available business contact details.
- **Address:** Your business address.
- **Logo URL:** The address of your business logo image.
- **Brand colors:** The colors configured for your business branding.

Review the existing information before making changes.

## Step 3: Update the details

Edit the fields you want to change.

Check spelling, contact details, and the address carefully. If you update the logo URL, make sure it points to an image that can be loaded by the application.

Only change fields that are displayed and editable in your current settings interface.

## Step 4: Save your changes

Use the save action provided by the business settings form.

Wait for Clientza to respond before leaving the page. If a success message or updated value is displayed, verify that it reflects the information you intended to save.

## Step 5: Check the saved information

Review the fields again to confirm that your changes were retained.

If a value has reverted or an error appears, use the troubleshooting steps below.

## Troubleshooting

### My changes did not save

Check for validation messages or an error displayed by Clientza. Correct any invalid fields and try saving again.

If the information still does not persist, reload the settings page and check whether the previous values remain. Avoid assuming a change was saved until you can verify it.

### My logo is not displayed

Check that the logo URL is correct and points to an image that can be loaded. If the image still does not appear, the URL or image resource may require investigation.

### I cannot find a setting

The settings page contains several different configuration areas. Review the available sections before assuming a setting is unavailable.

### The problem continues

Note the field you were updating and any error message shown by Clientza. This information will help identify the issue.
