---
title: "How to Create a Service Category"
description: "Organize your services into clear categories for easier booking and management."
---

Service categories help you group related services so owners and staff can find them quickly.

## Before you begin

Sign in to the Owner app and open the Services area.

## Steps

1. Open the service categories section.
2. Select the option to add a new category.
3. Enter a clear category name.
4. Add optional details if your screen provides them.
5. Save the category.

## Expected outcome

The new category appears in your services area and can be used when creating or updating services.

## Troubleshooting

### The category does not save

Check for required-field errors and duplicate names.

### I cannot find categories

Open the main Services page and look for category controls near the service list or creation form.
