---
title: "Settings Not Saving"
description: "Fix common issues when profile or business settings do not persist."
---

If a settings update does not persist, use this checklist.

## Step 1: Check required fields

A missing required field can block saving even if the edited field looks valid.

## Step 2: Check validation messages

Correct all inline errors before saving again.

## Step 3: Save and verify

After saving, reload the page and confirm the values persisted.

## Step 4: Isolate one change at a time

Make one small change and save. This helps identify which field is causing the failure.

## Step 5: Capture the exact error

If the issue continues, note the exact message and affected field for support follow-up.
