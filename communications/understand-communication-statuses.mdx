---
title: "Understand Communication Statuses"
description: "Interpret the status labels shown on communication drafts and records."
---

Status labels help you track which communication items still need action.

## How to review statuses

1. Open Communications.
2. Review each status label in the list.
3. Open specific records for details.

## Guidance

Use only statuses and actions that are visible in your current owner interface.

This Help Center does not assume delivery-provider behavior, send guarantees, or provider-specific delivery outcomes unless they are explicitly supported in your app.
