---
title: "How to Reschedule an Appointment"
description: "Move an existing appointment to a new date or time."
---

Rescheduling helps you keep bookings aligned with customer and provider availability.

## Steps

1. Open the appointment details.
2. Select the reschedule option.
3. Choose a new available time.
4. Review service and provider details.
5. Save the update.

## Expected outcome

The appointment moves to the selected slot and keeps an updated status in your list or calendar.

## Troubleshooting

### No new slots are available

Use [Troubleshoot Unavailable Appointment Times](/appointments/troubleshoot-unavailable-times).

### Appointment remains at old time

Refresh and verify the appointment details after saving.
