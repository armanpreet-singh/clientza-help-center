---
title: "How to Configure Payment Methods"
description: "Set up the payment methods your business accepts in Clientza."
---

Use payment settings to define which methods your business can record for customer payments.

## Steps

1. Open the Payments area.
2. Find payment method configuration.
3. Enable or configure the methods shown in your interface.
4. Save changes.

## Expected outcome

Payment methods are available when recording or reviewing customer-reported payments.

## Important note

Current documentation covers manual payment recording workflows.

Do not assume automated processing, payouts, refunds, card charging, or fee handling unless explicitly confirmed in your current product rollout.
