# How to Create a Clientza Owner Account


Create an owner account to start setting up your business with Clientza. Registration uses your name, email address, and a password, followed by email verification.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- An email address you can access.
- Your first and last name.
- A password that meets the requirements below.

## Step 1: Open the registration page

Open the Clientza Owner application and navigate to `/register`.

On the registration page, find the owner registration form.

## Step 2: Enter your personal details

Complete these fields:

1. **First name:** Enter your first name.
2. **Last name:** Enter your last name.
3. **Email address:** Enter the email address you want to use for your account.

Check that your email address is correct before continuing.

## Step 3: Create a password

Enter a password that meets all the requirements displayed on the registration form:

- At least 8 characters.
- No more than 128 characters.
- At least one uppercase letter.
- At least one lowercase letter.
- At least one number.
- At least one special character.
- No leading or trailing spaces.

Enter the same password in **Confirm password**.

Make sure both password fields match.

## Step 4: Accept the registration terms

Select the checkbox confirming that you agree to Clientza's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Review those documents before accepting. If either link is unavailable, do not assume the relevant policy is missing or that the link is working; the registration page's destinations should be verified.

## Step 5: Create your account

Click **Create account**.

Clientza submits your registration details and requests an email verification code.

If the form shows validation errors, correct the highlighted fields and check that all password requirements are satisfied.

## Step 6: Verify your email address

When the verification form appears:

1. Open the inbox for the email address you registered with.
2. Find the Clientza verification email.
3. Enter the verification code in **Enter verification code**.
4. Click **Verify email**.

The code must be accepted for registration to finish.

If you cannot find the email, check your Spam or Junk folder and confirm that you entered the correct email address. The current registration interface does not show a resend-code control.

## Step 7: Continue to business setup

After successful verification, Clientza finalizes registration and redirects you to the next authentication step.

If you have not yet created a business workspace, you will be directed to the initial business setup flow.

Continue with [How to Complete Your Initial Business Setup](/getting-started/initial-business-setup).

## Troubleshooting

### The Create account button is disabled

Check that all required fields are valid, both passwords match, and the terms checkbox is selected. Review the password requirements and correct any validation errors.

### My passwords do not match

Re-enter your password in **Confirm password**. Both values must match exactly.

### I did not receive a verification code

Check your inbox and Spam or Junk folder. Confirm that the email address you entered is correct. The current interface does not provide a visible option to resend the verification code, so this limitation may require attention if you cannot complete verification.

### Email verification did not complete

Make sure you entered the latest code you received and that it is correct. If verification still fails, the registration flow may need further investigation.

### I already have an account

Return to the Clientza login page at `/login` rather than creating another account.
