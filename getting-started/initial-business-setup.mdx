---
title: "How to Complete Your Initial Business Setup"
description: "Create your business workspace in Clientza and complete the initial setup process."
---

If you have signed in to Clientza but have not yet created a business workspace, the Owner application directs you to the initial business setup flow.

Completing this step creates your business workspace and its initial settings.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- A Clientza owner account.
- Access to the Owner application.
- The name of the business you want to set up.

## Step 1: Open the onboarding page

Sign in to the Clientza Owner application.

If your account does not yet have a business workspace, Clientza directs you to the onboarding page at `/onboarding/salon`.

Follow the setup form displayed on that page.

## Step 2: Enter your business details

Enter the business information requested by the form.

Check the details carefully before continuing.

## Step 3: Check the business URL slug

If the form asks you to choose a URL slug, enter the requested value and use the availability check provided by Clientza.

A slug identifies your business in its URL. If the chosen slug is unavailable, follow the form's validation message and choose another available value.

## Step 4: Submit the setup form

Review the information you entered and submit the form using the available action on the page.

Clientza validates the submitted information and creates the business workspace with its initial settings.

If the form displays an error, review the affected field and correct the information before trying again.

## Step 5: Continue to the Owner application

After the setup request succeeds, follow the next screen or navigation provided by Clientza.

The workspace should now exist for your owner account.

You can then continue configuring the business settings, services, and providers that are available in your dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot continue from onboarding

Review the form for validation messages. Correct any required or invalid fields and check the business URL slug if one is requested.

### My business URL slug is unavailable

Use the availability check provided by the form and choose a value that Clientza accepts.

### I see an error when submitting the form

Check the information you entered and try again after correcting any displayed validation errors. If the problem continues, note the message shown by Clientza so the issue can be investigated.

### I am redirected to onboarding every time I sign in

The application uses onboarding when an owner does not yet have a business workspace. If you believe your workspace has already been created, the repeated redirect may require investigation.
