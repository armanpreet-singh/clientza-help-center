---
title: "How to Cancel or Confirm an Appointment"
description: "Use available appointment actions to confirm bookings or cancel them when needed."
---

Use appointment actions to keep your schedule accurate.

## Steps

1. Open the Appointments page or Calendar.
2. Open the appointment details.
3. Choose the available action to confirm or cancel.
4. Add notes if your screen asks for a reason.
5. Save the change.

## Expected outcome

The appointment status updates and reflects your latest action.

## Troubleshooting

### Action buttons are not available

Availability can depend on current status. Open [Understand Appointment Statuses](/appointments/understand-appointment-statuses).

### Change did not persist

Refresh the page and verify status again.
