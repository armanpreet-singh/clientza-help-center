description: "Understand the current onboarding path for new Clientza owners, from consultation to invitation and business setup."
---

Clientza owner onboarding is invitation-based.

Instead of open self-registration, new owners usually begin by requesting a business consultation and then wait for an invitation after review.

## What to expect

1. You request a business consultation.
2. The Clientza team reviews your enquiry and speaks with you.
3. If approved, a platform administrator sends an owner invitation to your email address.
4. You open the invitation link and accept the invitation.
5. You create your password if this is your first Clientza account, or continue with sign-in if you already have an account.
6. After acceptance, you continue to initial business setup.
7. You complete setup and enter the Owner application.

## Current consultation-request limitation

The current consultation form is visible on the owner registration page, but submission is not fully connected yet.

The form currently shows a message that details have not been sent.

Do not treat a completed consultation form as submitted until this workflow is confirmed as operational.

## Consultation form fields currently shown

- Your name (required)
- Business name (required)
- Email (required)
- Phone
- Business address
- Type of business
- Team size
- Bookings a month
- When would you like to start?
- Moving from another system?
- Anything else?

## What to do now if you are onboarding

Follow the path offered in your invitation email once you receive it.

Continue with [How to Accept a Clientza Owner Invitation](/getting-started/accept-owner-invitation).

If you can already sign in, use [How to Sign In to Clientza](/getting-started/sign-in).

## Troubleshooting

### I submitted the consultation form, but nothing happened

At this time, the visible consultation form is not confirmed as connected submission.

Wait for the operational rollout, or follow the onboarding instructions shared directly by the Clientza team.

### I received an invitation email

Use the invitation link and follow [How to Accept a Clientza Owner Invitation](/getting-started/accept-owner-invitation).

### I already have a Clientza account

Use the existing-account option on the invitation flow when offered, then sign in.
