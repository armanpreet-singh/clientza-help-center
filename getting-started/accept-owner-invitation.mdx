---
title: "How to Accept a Clientza Owner Invitation"
description: "Accept your owner invitation, create a password if needed, and continue to business setup."
---

Use this guide when you receive an owner invitation email from Clientza.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- Access to the invited email inbox.
- The invitation email from Clientza.
- A stable internet connection.

## Step 1: Open your invitation email

Open the invitation email sent to the approved address.

Select the invitation link in the email.

## Step 2: Confirm the invited account

Follow the prompts on the invitation page.

The invitation flow confirms that the invited email is already verified for this process.

You do not need to enter a separate manual email-verification code during invitation acceptance.

## Step 3: Continue with the right path

Use the path shown on your screen:

1. New to Clientza: create your password and continue.
2. Existing account: choose the existing-account sign-in path if offered, then sign in.

## Step 4: Complete invitation acceptance

Finish all required fields and submit the invitation form.

Wait for confirmation that your invitation has been accepted.

## Step 5: Continue to initial business setup

After successful acceptance, continue to business setup if prompted.

Use [How to Complete Your Initial Business Setup](/getting-started/initial-business-setup).

## Expected outcome

Your owner invitation is accepted, and your account can continue into the Owner application setup flow.

## Troubleshooting

### My invitation link does not work

Open [Invitation Link Problems](/troubleshooting/invitation-link-problems) and follow the checks there.

### I already have an account

Use the existing-account option in the invitation flow when it appears, then sign in.

If you are redirected to sign-in, use [How to Sign In to Clientza](/getting-started/sign-in).

### I cannot complete setup after accepting

If onboarding starts, continue with [How to Complete Your Initial Business Setup](/getting-started/initial-business-setup).

If onboarding does not appear and you cannot continue, capture the on-screen message and contact Clientza support through your normal support channel.
