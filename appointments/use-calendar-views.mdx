---
title: "How to Use Calendar Views"
description: "Switch between available calendar views to review and manage appointments."
---

Calendar views help you plan daily and weekly scheduling.

## Steps

1. Open Calendar.
2. Switch between available views on your screen.
3. Select a date range.
4. Open appointments directly from the calendar.
5. Use available actions to review or update bookings.

## Expected outcome

You can review appointments visually and act on schedule changes faster.

## Troubleshooting

### The calendar does not show expected appointments

Check your selected date range and filters.

### I cannot edit from calendar

Open the appointment details and use the actions provided there.
