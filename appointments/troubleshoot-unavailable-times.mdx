---
title: "Troubleshoot Unavailable Appointment Times"
description: "Find and fix the most common reasons a time slot cannot be selected."
---

If you cannot choose an appointment time, check each dependency in order.

## Step 1: Check business schedule settings

Confirm regular business hours and date-specific overrides in settings.

- [Configure Your Business Hours](/business-settings/configure-business-hours)
- [Manage Date-Specific Availability Overrides](/business-settings/manage-date-overrides)

## Step 2: Check provider setup

Confirm the provider is active and assigned to the selected service.

- [Add and Update Providers](/providers/add-provider)
- [Assign Providers to Services](/services/assign-providers)

## Step 3: Check appointment conflicts

The selected slot may already be booked or blocked by existing appointments.

Try another nearby slot.

## Step 4: Re-check service details

Confirm service duration and assignment are valid for the selected date and provider.

## Expected outcome

You can identify the reason for unavailable times and select a valid slot.
