---
title: "How to Configure Your Business Hours"
description: "Set and save the regular weekly business hours used by your Clientza workspace."
---

Configure your regular business hours in Clientza's business settings. These hours are part of the availability information used by the appointment scheduling workflow.

## Before you begin

Sign in to the Clientza Owner dashboard and open `/settings`.

Review the time zone configured for your business before adjusting the schedule.

## Step 1: Open business settings

Navigate to `/settings` in the Owner application.

Find the business-hours section.

## Step 2: Review the weekly schedule

Review the schedule for each day of the week.

For every day, use the controls available in your settings form to indicate whether your business is open and to specify the opening and closing times when applicable.

Make sure the schedule reflects your intended regular hours.

## Step 3: Check the times carefully

Before saving, review the opening and closing times for each day.

- Make sure the entered times are valid.
- Check that the opening time is earlier than the closing time wherever the form requires a same-day interval.
- Review every day rather than assuming that changing one day updates the rest of the week.
- Confirm that the configured business time zone is appropriate.

If a day should be closed, use the closed-day option if one is provided by the interface.

## Step 4: Save the schedule

Use the save action in the business-hours section.

Wait for Clientza to respond. If a success message or updated schedule is displayed, verify that the saved hours match your intended schedule.

The schedule is not confirmed as saved merely because the fields have been edited.

## Step 5: Verify your changes

Review the weekly schedule after saving.

If the values have reverted or an error appears, follow the troubleshooting steps below before relying on the new hours.

## Need to close your business on a specific date?

Regular business hours describe your weekly schedule. A one-off closure or a different schedule for a particular date is handled through availability overrides.

See [How to Manage Date-Specific Availability Overrides](/business-settings/manage-date-overrides).

## Troubleshooting

### My business hours did not save

Check for validation errors in the form, correct any invalid times, and try saving again.

If the problem continues, reload the settings page and check whether the previous schedule remains.

### The form rejects my schedule

Review all seven days of the weekly schedule. Clientza's backend validates the weekly schedule and expects seven distinct days with valid schedule data.

Correct the affected entries and save again.

### I need different hours for one date

Use the availability override controls in business settings rather than changing the regular weekly schedule solely to accommodate a one-off closure or special-hours period.

### I cannot find the business-hours controls

Make sure you are using `/settings`. The separate `/availability` page in the inspected application contains preview-only controls that do not save the production business-hours schedule.
