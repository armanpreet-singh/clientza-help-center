---
title: "How to Manage Date-Specific Availability Overrides"
description: "Add or remove a closure or special-hours override for a particular date in Clientza business settings."
---

Use a date-specific availability override when your business needs different hours on a particular date. For example, you may need to close for a day or use different opening and closing times for a special occasion.

Regular business hours remain configured separately.

## Before you begin

Sign in to the Clientza Owner dashboard and open `/settings`.

Check the business time zone and your regular weekly schedule before adding an override.

## Step 1: Open availability overrides

Navigate to `/settings` and find the availability overrides section.

Use this section for date-specific changes rather than the preview-only controls on the separate `/availability` page.

## Step 2: Add an override

Use the control provided by the settings page to add a date-specific override.

Select the date you want the override to apply to and provide the details requested by the form.

Depending on the options displayed, an override can represent a closure or a different opening and closing interval for that date.

## Step 3: Review the date and hours

Before saving, check that:

- The selected date is correct.
- The override represents the closure or special hours you intend.
- Any opening and closing times are valid.
- The times make sense for your business time zone.

Correct any errors displayed by the form before continuing.

## Step 4: Save the override

Use the save action provided by the settings page.

Wait for Clientza to respond. If the override appears in the saved list or a success message is displayed, verify that it applies to the intended date.

Do not assume the override was saved until you can verify it in the interface.

## Step 5: Review or remove an override

Return to the availability overrides section in `/settings` to review existing entries.

If the interface provides the option to delete an override, use that action when the date-specific change is no longer needed. Check the result to confirm that the entry has been removed.

Removing an override changes the date-specific exception; review the resulting schedule before relying on it.

## Troubleshooting

### My override did not save

Check the selected date and any required time fields. Correct any validation errors and try again.

If the override still does not appear in the saved list, reload the settings page and check whether it persisted.

### I need to change my regular weekly hours

Use the business-hours section instead of creating a date-specific override.

See [How to Configure Your Business Hours](/business-settings/configure-business-hours).

### I cannot find the availability controls

Open `/settings` in the Owner dashboard. The separate `/availability` page contains preview-only controls in the inspected application and does not persist production availability changes.

### An appointment time is unavailable

Availability depends on the configured schedule and the appointment rules applied by Clientza. Review the business hours, date-specific overrides, selected service, and provider availability.

See [Troubleshoot Unavailable Appointment Times](/appointments/troubleshoot-unavailable-times).
